The Manokara Folia output bundles the upstream Folia visualizer renderers. The upstream source
archive is provided as FOLIA-MAJOR-SOURCE.zip; the license is in FOLIA-MAJOR-LICENSE.txt. The
Manokara bridge/build source is in FOLIA-INTEGRATION-SOURCE, and the browser bundle is in
folia-assets with its entry page at manokara-folia.html.

The Folia repository README describes the project/source as intended for personal learning,
technical exchange, and non-profit testing, and asks users not to use it for commercial profit.
This integration is intended for local personal karaoke/OBS use. Preserve these notices and review
AGPL-3.0 obligations before redistributing or exposing a combined Manokara/Folia service to others.
